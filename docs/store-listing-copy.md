## Full description

### English

KEKE Manager is a B2B platform connecting transport companies and tour operators with professional, verified drivers across Georgia.

**For transport & tour companies:**
- Create and manage transfer, tour, and multi-day tour bookings
- Get matched with available, verified drivers and vehicles
- Track your driver live on the map during an active trip
- Chat directly with assigned drivers
- Rate completed trips and build a trusted driver network
- Generate ready-to-share vouchers for clients

**For professional drivers:**
- Accept bookings that match your vehicle and schedule
- Simple document and vehicle verification, done once
- Odometer photo capture at the start/end of tours for accurate accounting
- Job board for hired drivers without their own vehicle
- Push notifications for new bookings and messages
- Manage your fleet if you host sub-drivers

**Built for trust and accountability:**
- Every driver and vehicle is verified before going live
- Vehicle photos must be refreshed periodically to stay active
- Live GPS is only ever shared during an active, assigned trip — never at rest
- In-app account deletion, full data control

KEKE Manager is a software platform only — not a transport carrier or employer. Trip safety, licensing, and insurance remain the responsibility of the companies and drivers using the platform.

Available in Georgian, English, Russian, and Armenian.
