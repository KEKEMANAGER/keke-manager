## 3. Description of the Service

KEKE Manager is a B2B platform that connects **transport companies** with **professional drivers** to manage:

- Transfer, tour, and day-tour **bookings**  
- **Driver matching** by vehicle type, class, and languages  
- **Live GPS tracking** during active trips  
- **In-app chat** and **push notifications**  
- **Driver verification** (identity and vehicle documents)  
- **Ratings** after completed trips  
- **Fleet** management (host drivers and sub-drivers)  
- **Job board** for hired drivers seeking work  
- **Administrative** verification and moderation tools  

We do not guarantee availability of drivers or bookings in all regions or at all times.
