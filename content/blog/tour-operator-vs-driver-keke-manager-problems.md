## English

Georgia's tourism industry grows every season — but transport management still relies on outdated methods. Tour operators coordinate drivers through WhatsApp groups, while drivers get lost in dozens of messages. **KEKE Manager** was built to solve exactly this chaos.

## The tour operator's problems

Every tour means hours on the phone:

- Who's going?
- Which driver?
- Where's the vehicle?
- Flight delayed — who picks up?

Answers often arrive late — or not at all. A spreadsheet, a WhatsApp “confirmed”, and a frantic airport call rarely tell the same story.

**The result:** frustrated tourists, damaged reputation, and emergency payments to whoever answers first.

### What KEKE Manager gives companies

One dashboard where you see:

- All bookings (transfer, multi-day tour, day tour)
- Driver status — pending, accepted, in progress
- **Real-time GPS** during active trips
- **PDF vouchers** generated automatically

Creating a new booking takes about three minutes — no phone calls. Drivers get push alerts and accept or reject in one tap. You see where the vehicle is without calling anyone.

## The driver's problems

A professional driver starts every morning waiting — **will anyone call?** Work is unpredictable; income is unstable. Summer is overloaded; winter is quiet.

A WhatsApp “you free tomorrow?” is not a contract. There is no history and no guarantee the verbal deal will hold.

### What KEKE Manager gives drivers

A platform where you:

- See **open bookings** matched to your vehicle type, class, and languages
- **Choose** work instead of waiting for calls
- Manage your **schedule** — calendar, active and completed trips
- Get **vouchers** on your phone — PDF with route and client details
- Earn a **verified badge** — companies consistently prefer vetted drivers

**29 GEL per month** — roughly the cost of a single transfer. One solid airport run often pays for the subscription.

## One platform — two sides

KEKE Manager is more than a booking system. It is a **bridge** between companies and drivers:

| Feature | Company | Driver |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Bookings | Create, edit | Accept / reject |
| GPS | Live tracking | Background location on trip |
| Voucher | PDF for clients | On phone, share |
| Chat | Company ↔ driver | Same thread |
| Ratings | Rate the driver | Build reputation |
| Payment | Confirm paid | IBAN, payout |

**Fleet hosts** manage sub-drivers, fleet map, and “I'll drive vs assign driver”. **Hired drivers** use the host's vehicle and assigned tours only.

## Pricing — transparent

- **Tour operator / company:** platform is **completely free**
- **Driver / fleet host:** **29 GEL / month** — about one transfer, full network access

No commission cut from the tour price. Companies and drivers set their own rates.
