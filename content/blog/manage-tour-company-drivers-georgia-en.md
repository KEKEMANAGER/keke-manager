Tour companies in Georgia face the same challenge every day: coordinating drivers, distributing bookings, and monitoring active tours in real time. This process typically runs through WhatsApp messages, phone calls, or paper vouchers — making efficiency nearly impossible to achieve.

## A unified platform — all bookings in one place

KEKE Manager gives tour operators a single system to manage all their drivers. Sending, confirming, and cancelling bookings is handled automatically, with a full record kept at every step.

## GPS tracking — know exactly where your driver is

When a tourist's flight is delayed, the company operator can see the driver's exact location through GPS and update the route immediately — no frantic phone calls required.

## Digital vouchers — no more paper

Every booking automatically generates a digital voucher. The driver sees it on their phone; the company sees it on their dashboard. Simple, fast, and fully traceable.

## Conclusion

KEKE Manager is the first B2B platform in Georgia built specifically for tour operators and drivers. In 2025, digital fleet management is not a luxury — it is a competitive necessity.
